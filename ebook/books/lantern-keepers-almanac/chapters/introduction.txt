Introduction

An almanac is a strange kind of book. It promises to tell you what the year will bring: the tides, the frosts, the days when the sun rises early. Yet every almanac is also a little bit wrong, because the world refuses to follow a schedule for long.

## How This Book Is Arranged

The chapters that follow are organized by season rather than by subject. Each one begins with a short observation, continues with a few stories, and ends with a small task you can try for yourself, such as lighting a candle at the same hour for a week.

## A Note on Method

Nothing in these pages requires special equipment. A window, a notebook, and a willingness to sit still for ten minutes will carry you through every chapter. Where I recommend a tool, I also describe a cheaper or simpler alternative.

Let us begin where the year begins, in the dark.
